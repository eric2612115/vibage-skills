Impl freeze record for v0.9.3.4 — everything under `.github/` is CI definition unless it is
listed repo metadata, and CI-suite discovery reads composite actions through one shared
function.

Trigger set (gate class, N=2): `scripts/lib/review_record.py`, `tests/test_review_record.sh`,
`references/review-budget.md`, `references/looping-review.md`.

Before this change, a composite action under `.github/actions/**` running a suite, called
from a new workflow, added an ungoverned CI job with no record and no red check — the one
disclosed residual that cost nothing to use. Both layers now fire: the record gate on the
changed CI files, and the derivation on the hidden suite name.

`REVIEW_RECORD_OK` here means the record is schema-valid. It is not a claim about review
quality, and reviewer model/context/selection are self-declared and unverifiable. Gating CI
definition forces a record; it does not guarantee a reviewer notices a job that quietly
stops running something.
